## Brand & Style

The design system projects uncompromised architectural luxury, engineering precision, and permanence. Crafted for ultra-luxury residential clients, master interior architects, commercial restaurateurs, and hospitality developers, the interface communicates the technical prowess of invisible stone protection without compromising the organic beauty of rare marble and quartzite.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Minimalism** with **Warm Luxury Modernism**. High-contrast typography, deep slate backgrounds, crystalline highlights, and delicate champagne metallic accents evoke the atmosphere of an elite design showroom. Structural precision, generous negative space, and disciplined layout grids reflect the exactitude of custom fabrication and architectural detailing.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental mineral weight with luminous luxury accents. 

- **Primary (`#CA8A04`) & Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Warm champagne gold and amber brass accents denote certified craftsmanship, premium guarantee badges, warranties, and key calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`) & Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: Form the structural foundation. Deep slate navy and honed charcoal provide depth, high-end drama, and an intimate backdrop that emphasizes polished stone textures.
- **Pristine Marble White (`#F8FAFC`)**: Applied strictly for high-clarity primary typography and crisp technical readouts.
- **Stone Cool Gray (`#64748B`)**: Reserved for secondary architectural specs, hair-line borders, and secondary copy.

The dark mode default delivers an immersive, evening-showroom environment that highlights high-gloss photography and durability credentials.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial architectural cadence. 

- **Headlines (Outfit)**: Geometric yet refined, with sculpted proportions that echo contemporary stone slab geometry. Large display scales use tight letter-spacing to emphasize weight and authority.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Clean, utilitarian, and neutral. Inter provides razor-sharp legibility for warranty descriptions, technical thermal ratings, and architectural finish guides.
- **Micro-Copy & Specifications (`label-caps`)**: All-caps tracking with wide spacing (`0.15em`) creates a blueprint-like precision for warranties, material classifications, and technical figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- Generous horizontal and vertical negative space balances heavy slabs of visual imagery with rigorous technical metrics.
- Component-level spacing follows an 8-point structural scale with half-steps (`0.25rem`) used for compact badge styling.
- Layout sections maintain clear architectural delineation using proportional vertical breaks: section margins scale from `space-lg` on compact views to `space-xl` and higher on wide displays to ensure an uncrowded, curated presentation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **tonal layering** and **low-contrast architectural outlines**:

- **Surfaces**: Canvas begins at deep slate navy (`#0F172A`). Cards and elevated panels step up to honed charcoal (`#1E293B`) or a 40% translucent blend with backdrop blur over imagery.
- **Outlines**: Floating cards, spec tables, and interactive panels use crisp, hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.25)`) echoing edge-profile fabrication drawings.
- **Shadows**: Physical drop shadows are avoided in favor of subtle, warm-tinted ambient glow effects (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.7)`) underneath elevated showcases. Interactive elements gain a hairline champagne accent highlight upon hover or activation.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Radius is restrained to `0.25rem` (4px) for standard UI controls and `0.5rem` (8px) for major containment cards.

This crisp radius preserves the tactile, slab-like appearance of polished architectural stone, rejecting overly playful or circular aesthetics in favor of structural permanence and precision-cut edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid champagne gold (`#CA8A04`) surface, deep charcoal text (`#0F172A`), uppercase label tracking, `4px` radius. Hover shifts to `#D97706` with subtle brass aura.
- **Secondary (Architectural Outline)**: Transparent base, 1px perimeter border in `#64748B` or `#CA8A04`, pristine white text. Hover fills to `rgba(202, 138, 4, 0.08)`.
- **Destructive/Critical**: Muted slate crimson with low-saturation highlight.

### Chips & Badges
- **Guarantee Badges (e.g., "10-Year Warranty", "Heat Resistant 400°F")**: Compact inline elements featuring `1px` border in `#CA8A04` at 40% opacity, slate fill (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`), and `#F8FAFC` caps typography.
- **Filter Chips**: Minimalist slab buttons with pill-like restraint (`4px` radius), inactive in cool stone gray, active with solid slate charcoal and champagne indicator pip.

### Cards
- **Specification & Project Showcase Cards**: Honed charcoal fill (`#1E293B`), hairline perimeter (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.2)`), inner padding `space-lg`. Media headers feature zero radius at seams to maintain slab integrity.

### Input Fields
- Form inputs feature subtle dark slate backgrounds (`#0F172A`), `1px` stone borders (`#64748B`), and crisp white typography. Focused states transition the border to clean champagne gold (`#CA8A04`) with no aggressive outer glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square and disc forms with crisp 1px borders in `#64748B`. Checked states fill with solid `#CA8A04` and slate navy iconography.

### Stone Performance Split Comparison
- Interactive dual-layer comparison sliders for demonstration of etched/raw marble versus invisible laminate protection. Framed by razor-thin slate dividers with minimal champagne center-draggers.
