Flawed Chemistry vs Physical Armor

Why Traditional Penetrating Sealers Inevitably Fail

Natural marble is predominantly calcium carbonate (CaCO₃)—an alkaline mineral hypersensitive to acids. Traditional impregnating sealers only slow fluid absorption into micro-pores. They leave the mineral surface completely naked to chemical reaction.

The moment lemon juice, red wine, balsamic vinegar, or cleaning agents touch sealed stone, an irreversible chemical ionization occurs in under 3 seconds, dissolving the calcite face into rough, dull etch craters.
