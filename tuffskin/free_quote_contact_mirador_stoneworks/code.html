Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Houston homeowners and designers need to know before an in-home estimate.

How quickly can Mirador Stoneworks install after the quote?

expand_more
Most Houston residential kitchens take approximately 4 to 6 hours to complete in a single visit with zero odor, zero dust, and zero messy chemical fumes. Our scheduling queue is typically within 3 to 7 business days from deposit approval.

Can you remove existing etch marks before installing TuffSkin?

expand_more
Yes. Mirador Stoneworks provides comprehensive restorative diamond honing and scratch remediation prior to laminate application. We restore your marble to flawless clarity before locking it permanently beneath TuffSkin.

Is there any difference between Gloss and Satin finishes?

expand_more
Both offer identical 10-year chemical protection and 400°F heat resistance. Gloss enhances depth and reflection on polished marble, while Satin delivers a soft, tactile, low-sheen look ideal for honed surfaces.
